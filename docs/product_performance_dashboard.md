# Products Performance Dashboard

![Products Performance Dashboar](https://github.com/CHodes-dev/SQL-Data-Analysis-Project/blob/main/docs/images/products_dashboard.png)

## 1. Introduction

This dashboard provides an overview of product performance from the [report_products](https://github.com/CHodes-dev/SQL-Data-Analysis-Project/blob/main/script/report_products.sql). It monitors sales, revenue, costs, quantities and product performance segments to identify high-performing products and assess their contribution at category level.

## 2. Dashboard Metrics

The dashboard includes eight Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
| KPI | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Total Products | Measures the number of distinct products included in the analysis. |
| Total Sales | Represents the total sales value generated by all products. |
| Total Orders | Measures the total number of orders recorded during the analysis period. |
| Total Quantity | Represents the total number of product units sold. |
| Average Order Revenue | Shows the average revenue generated per order, helping evaluate the value of each transaction. |
| Average Monthly Revenue | Measures the average revenue generated per month to monitor revenue performance over time. |
| Average Selling Price | Shows the average selling price of products, helping evaluate product pricing levels. |
| Average Cost | Represents the average cost associated with products, providing a basis for evaluating cost efficiency. |

## 3. Product Analysis

The dashboard provides three views of product performance:

- Product Segmentation: Groups products into performance categories to identify high-performing products and understand the overall distribution of product performance.
- Category Revenue and Cost: Compares revenue and cost across product categories to evaluate their financial contributions and identify categories with high revenue or cost.
- Top 10 Products by Sales: Ranks products based on total sales and compares their associated costs to understand differences in sales contribution and cost structure.

## 4. Key Findings

- **Category Contribution**: Bikes generate the highest revenue, with large margins as well.
- **Product Performance**: Half of all products already fall into the ‘high-performer’ category. These high-performing products need to be prioritised whilst continuing to boost sales of products in other segments.
- **Top-Selling Products**: The six highest-ranked products have lower costs than the four products ranked below them. Further analysis should compare revenue and cost on a consistent basis to determine which products deliver better profit margins.

## 5. Conclusion

The dashboard highlights differences in product performance, category contribution, and cost structure. These insights can support decisions on product prioritization, cost management, and category-level evaluation. Further analysis of profit margins and product-level profitability can help determine which products contribute the most financial value to the business.
