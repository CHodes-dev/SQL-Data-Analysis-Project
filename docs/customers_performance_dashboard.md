# Customers Performance Dashboard

!(https://github.com/CHodes-dev/SQL-Data-Analysis-Project/blob/main/docs/images/customers_dashboard.png)

## 1. Introduction

This dashboard presents a visualisation of the data from the analysis results in the previously created ‘customer_report’. It is designed to monitor sales performance, purchasing behaviour, customer value and customer distribution in order to support the evaluation of customer performance.

## 2. Dashboard Metrics

The dashboard includes six Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):
| KPI | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Total Sales | Total revenue generated from customer purchases. |
| Total Orders | Total number of orders placed by customers. |
| Average Order Value (AOV) | Average sales value per order. |
| Average Recency | Average time since customers' last purchases. |
| Average Monthly Spend | Average customer spending per month. |
| Average Lifespan | Average duration of the customer relationship. |

## 3. Customer Analysis

The dashboard provides additional views to understand customer characteristics and purchasing behavior:
- **Customer Segmentation**: Groups customers by age and membership status to compare customer distribution across different groups.
- **Recent Order Trends**: Shows fluctuations in the number of customers' most recent orders to identify changes in purchasing activity.
- **Top 5 Customers**: Highlights the five customers with the highest purchase totals to identify high-value customers.

## 4. Key Findings

- Sales Performance: The combination of high total sales and order volume indicates substantial overall sales activity, while AOV provides a baseline for evaluating the value of each transaction.
- Customer Behavior: Average recency suggesting that customers' last purchases were a long time ago, this may indicate a customer reactivation opportunity. And, average customer lifespan relatively short, retention strategies could help extend customer relationships.
- Customer Segments: Customer base is concentrated among older (>50 years old) customers. And, Most customers are 'New', highlighting an opportunity to improve customer conversion and loyalty.
- Top Customers: The highest-spending customer has a lifespan of 27 months, suggesting that meaningful customer value can be generated over a relatively limited customer relationship period.

## 5. Conclusion

The dashboard provides an overview of customer performance, purchasing behavior, and customer value from 2010 to 2014. These insights can support customer evaluation, identify valuable customer segments, and inform strategies to improve customer retention and sales performance.
